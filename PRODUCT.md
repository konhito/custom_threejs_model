# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers, children, and families discovering the Nahiara Universe, including returning readers opening purchased chapters across devices.

## Product Purpose

AquaMundi presents the Nahiara universe and its educational book, card game, ecology, and digital chapter library. The library must make progression and reading immediately clear.

## Brand Personality

Warm, botanical, editorial. The experience should feel protective of the canon while remaining welcoming and joyful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic commerce inventory language, cramped labels, and dark dashboard-like reading interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Let reading and the story lead every library decision.
- Make ecological learning feel inviting, never instructional overload.
- Keep progress visible without making ownership feel transactional.
- Match the established shop typography and editorial rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast and touch targets, preserve reduced-motion support, and avoid relying on colour alone for chapter availability.
