🐝 2. ROL COMO POLINIZADOR 🐝 2. ROLE AS POLLINATOR

Relación con el Clavel: Relationship with the Carnation:

Los lepidópteros, especialmente las polillas esfíngidas, son polinizadores clave para flores con corolas largas y tubulares como el Clavel. Las polillas nocturnas se sienten atraídas por la intensa fragancia que el clavel emite durante la noche. Su larga espiritrompa les permite alcanzar el néctar profundo, inaccesible para otros insectos. Lepidoptera, especially hawkmoths, are key pollinators for flowers with long, tubular corollas like the Carnation. Nocturnal moths are attracted to the intense fragrance that the carnation emits at night. Their long proboscis allows them to reach the deep nectar, inaccessible to other insects.

The order Lepidoptera integrates into the Nahiara universe with code SAN-P-12125, evaluated with a level 18 (Stable) in the Araihan Measure. This diverse group of insects, which includes butterflies and moths, plays a crucial role as pollinators in almost all terrestrial ecosystems. Their relationship with flowers like the Carnation, especially through hawkmoths, is an example of co-evolution and specialization. However, the fragility of their populations in the face of habitat loss, pesticides and light pollution makes them a sensitive indicator of environmental health. Protecting lepidoptera is essential to maintain the genetic diversity of plants and the stability of food webs.
